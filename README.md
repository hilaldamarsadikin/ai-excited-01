A single electrifying image for the make.fun **ai-excited-01** bounty: a tiger's
face in extreme close-up, split between electric cyan and hot magenta neon
light, with glowing eyes staring straight at the viewer against a black
background. One subject, instantly readable in a second, built to stand out
when judged side by side with other images.

Four concepts were generated and compared (phoenix, neon tiger, cosmic
jellyfish, volcano + lightning); the neon tiger won for its instant
readability, eye contact, and maximum color contrast. The other candidates
are kept in [`options/`](options/).

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) copies `entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB)
into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).
